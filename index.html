Session 1 — Control & Dispersion

60–90 min
Warm-up10 min

PW/9i → 7i. Smooth swings, clear targets, no mechanical thoughts.

Iron direction15 min

5-shot sets at 150–180 yd. Track green hits and left/right dispersion.

Driver control20–25 min

Full driver vs controlled driver in 5-ball sets. Score playable/not playable. Finish with 5 one-ball pressure tee shots.

56° contact15 min

60–70 yd. Clean predictable contact before exact yardage.

Putting maintenance10 min

4–5 footers, then 20/30/40-foot lag putts. Include downhill breakers.
